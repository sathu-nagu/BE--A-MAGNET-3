04 / GROWTH

Personal Growth

Recognise your strengths and take small steps toward the life you want to create.

A THOUGHT TO EXPLORE

Progress is built one step at a time.

Personal growth is a continuing process of learning about yourself, recognising your strengths and choosing practical steps towards meaningful goals.

SIMPLE PRACTICES

Start with small steps

  • Identify one strength you can use today.
  • Choose one small, realistic goal.
  • At the end of the day, reflect on what you learned.
YOUR ONE-MINUTE PRACTICE

Take a moment for yourself

Choose one goal you care about and make the first step so small that you can begin it today.
